The decisions baked into it

🔁

One month, one invoice

The retainer row carries the period it was last billed for. Run the billing twice and the second run bills nothing — and says exactly that, per row.

⏱

Billing day, not just "the 1st"

Half your clients pay on the 1st, half on the 28th. Each row holds its own day, so a mid-month run bills only what is actually due — and the email says what's coming within 3 days.

📈

Overage you can defend

Promised 20h, logged 22h → the invoice shows the 2h and the rate. Log 22h against a 40h deal and it bills nothing extra and shows 55% utilisation, which is the conversation you should be having instead.

🔓

MRR that isn't inflated

A retainer that ended on 31 Aug or starts in October is real, but it isn't this month's recurring revenue. In force means live: 6 of the 8 rows marked Active, ₹4,20,000 not ₹5,00,000.

🤫

Quiet, not churned

The client who stops emailing you isn't a red flag in a CRM — they're ₹60,000 a month walking away. Days since the last logged hour is the earliest honest signal you have.

🧩

It knows what it isn't

Not a timesheet, not a payment processor, not GST advice, and it won't chase the client who hasn't paid. It bills the retainer; the Invoice Kit chases the money.
