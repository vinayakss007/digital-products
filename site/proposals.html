From "we discussed it" to "pay the deposit"

1

New Draft Proposal

Takes the next number (PROP-001, 002, …), stamps today, and sets the expiry from how long you want your price to hold.

2

Write the scope plainly

One line per item: Website build | 40 | 1500. Quantity and rate are optional — a line of plain text prices at zero so you can list inclusions without maths.

3

Recalculate Totals

Subtotal, tax at your rate, total, and the deposit your Settings percentage produces. Written back to the row.

4

Email Proposal With PDF

Generates the PDF into its own Drive folder, attaches that file, states the total and the deposit, and moves the row to Sent. Confirm dialog first.

5

Move To Accepted

One click from the stage submenu. It stamps the acceptance date, logs the change, and refreshes the dashboard.

6

Convert To Invoice

Creates a numbered draft on the Invoices tab — the deposit only, or the whole scope if you don't take deposits. Nothing to retype.

The decisions baked into it

⏳

Proposals expire on purpose

Every document carries a validity date, and a lapsed one is visible as an amount, not a vibe. "Still waiting" is not a pipeline stage.

🧟

Win rate excludes zombies

A proposal nobody answered is not a "no". Expired rows sit outside the win rate so the number reflects deals you actually competed for.

💰

Deposit first, always

Calculated on every proposal and printed on the PDF. The convert step bills the deposit by default, so "we're agreed" never becomes unpaid work.

📧

Chase without discounting

The reminder email offers to move scope, not to drop price — because shrinking the work is usually the honest fix and a discount isn't.

🎯

You set the probabilities

Win chances live on a tab, not in the code. If Negotiating is 60% for you, change one cell and the pipeline maths follows.

🧩

It knows what it isn't

No e-signatures, no payment gateway, no legal language. It gets a priced document in front of a client and a yes into your invoice queue.
