What it actually does

Not a prettier list. A list that runs.

📧

Follow-up reminders by email

Every morning it checks which leads are overdue and emails you the list. This is the feature that pays for the template.

💎

Weighted pipeline value

A ₹2,00,000 proposal at 60% is not ₹2,00,000 of revenue. It forecasts on stage probability so you can plan on real money.

🔄

One-click stage moves

Move a lead from Qualified to Proposal from a menu. It stamps the date, reschedules the follow-up and logs the change.

📊

Dashboard that rebuilds itself

Pipeline value, average deal size, conversion rate and deals lost — recalculated on demand, no formulas to maintain.

📝

Activity log per lead

Every interaction recorded with a timestamp and who logged it, so you know what you last said to each person.

🏷️

Source and tag tracking

Find out whether referrals or cold outreach actually produces revenue, instead of guessing.

Setup, honestly

1

Create a sheet and paste the script

Extensions → Apps Script → paste → save. Five clicks.

2

Run setup once

Creates the Leads, Pipeline, Activities, Dashboard and Settings tabs with headers filled in.

3

Set your email and follow-up interval

Two fields on the Settings tab.

4

Add a daily trigger

One dropdown in the Apps Script triggers panel. This is what makes the reminders automatic.

5

Paste in your real leads

Sample data included so you can see it working before you commit your own.

Roughly five minutes if you have used Google Sheets before.
