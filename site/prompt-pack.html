The six personas

Paste one at the top of a chat and every prompt gets sharper, because the model already knows your business and your standards.

🧭

The Operator

Your business partner. Challenges the plan before executing it, refuses to invent statistics, keeps answers short.

✒️

The Copywriter

Problem first, one idea per piece, a banned-words list, and every claim either yours or marked [VERIFY].

🤨

The Skeptic

Reads your draft as a busy, distrustful buyer. Finds the three things that make them close the tab.

📐

The Analyst

Restates what your numbers say before interpreting them, separates fact from inference, and names the one metric you are missing.

🗂️

The Systems Builder

Designs processes with one owner and one trigger, and always writes the failure case: what happens when someone skips a step.

🎓

The Teacher

Explains your product to someone intelligent with five minutes of patience. Analogy first, then the steps, then what to do next.

The four-message loop worth memorising
1. Paste the Operator persona
2. Run any prompt from the pack
3. Paste the result into the Skeptic and get 5 objections
4. "Rewrite it fixing exactly those 5, same length."
